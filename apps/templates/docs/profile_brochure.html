Welcome to
JamiiTek.

JamiiTek is a Tanzanian digital agency. We design and build websites, management systems, mobile apps and WhatsApp automation for schools, NGOs, tour operators, lenders and growing businesses — in English and Kiswahili, built for mobile phones and mobile money.

We take on a small number of clients at a time, so each one gets a finished, working product and a team that stays on after launch for hosting, updates and support.

Mission — to give every Tanzanian organisation digital tools that win customers, save time and earn trust.

Vision — to be East Africa's most trusted digital partner.
