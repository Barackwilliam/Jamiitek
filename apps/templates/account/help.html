Signing in
I forgot my password

Go to Reset your password, enter the email on your account and open the link we send you. The link works once and expires in 60 minutes.

Your new password works on every JamiiTek app straight away.

It says my account is locked

After several wrong passwords we pause sign-in for 15 minutes to stop anyone guessing it. You can:

  • wait 15 minutes and try again, or
  • reset your password — this unlocks the account immediately.
I didn't receive the reset email
  • Wait 2–3 minutes, then check Spam, Promotions and Updates.
  • Make sure you typed the email that is on your account.
  • For security you can request up to 5 emails per hour.

Still nothing? Contact us below and we'll help you sign in.

Can I sign in with my email instead of my username?

Yes. The Client Portal, JamiiBot and Web Builder sign-in pages accept either your username or the email address on your account.

Two-step verification
Where do I find the 6-digit code?

Open the authenticator app you used during setup (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or Authy) and look for JamiiTek. The code changes every 30 seconds — type the one currently shown, then wait a moment; the page continues by itself.

My code is not accepted
  1. Use the newest code — don't reuse one you already entered.
  2. Check that your phone's time is set automatically (Settings → Date & time → Automatic). A clock that is off by a minute or more makes codes fail.
  3. Make sure you are reading the JamiiTek entry in the app.

After 5 wrong codes you are signed out for safety — just sign in again.

I lost my phone or changed phones

On the verification page choose Use a recovery code and enter one of the recovery codes you saved during setup. Each code works once.

Once you're in, connect your new phone and create fresh recovery codes so the old ones stop working.

I have no phone and no recovery codes

Contact us using the details below. To protect your account we'll confirm your identity before resetting two-step verification — we will never ask for your password or codes.

Staying safe
I think someone else used my account
  1. Reset your password now — this signs out every other device.
  2. Contact us so we can review recent activity with you.
Someone asked me for my password or code

Don't share it. JamiiTek staff will never ask for your password, verification codes or recovery codes — by phone, SMS, email or WhatsApp. Report the message to us.
