Before you connect: two devices

10:16

WhatsApp links a bot the same way it links WhatsApp Web — by scanning a QR code. You scan with one phone while the code shows on another screen.

1

Your business phone

The phone with the WhatsApp number your customers already message. This is the number the bot answers from.

  • WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business installed
  • You use it to scan the QR code (Linked devices)
  • Keep it charged and online at least once every 14 days
2

A second phone (or a computer)

Open your JamiiBot dashboard here to show the QR code — a phone cannot scan its own screen.

  • Shows the QR code while phone 1 scans it
  • Then send your bot a test message from it
  • A laptop or tablet works too
  1. Phone 2: sign in to JamiiBot → Connect WhatsApp → a QR code appears.
  2. Phone 1: WhatsApp → ⋮ / Settings → Linked devices → Link a device → scan the code.
  3. Done ✅ — send “Habari” to your business number from phone 2 and watch the bot reply.
